# Manager Role User Guide

## Overview
The **Manager** role allows you to oversee your assigned team members. You can view their attendance, manage leave requests, and monitor team performance.

## Accessing the Dashboard
- Log in with your credentials.
- You will be automatically redirected to the **Manager Dashboard** (`/manager`).
- If you are an Admin, you can switch views or access the manager dashboard directly.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Team Overview
- **Total Team Members**: Number of employees reporting to you.
- **Present Today**: Real-time count of team members currently checked in.
- **Pending Leaves**: Number of leave requests awaiting your approval.

### 2. Team Attendance
- View a list of your team members.
- See their check-in/check-out times for the current day.
- **Status Indicators**:
  - 🟢 Present
  - 🔴 Absent
  - 🟡 On Leave
  - ⚪ Not Checked In

### 3. Leave Management
- Navigate to the **Leave Requests** section (or "Review Leave Requests" in Quick Actions).
- You will see requests **only from your team members**.
- **Actions**:
  - **Approve**: Grant the leave request.
  - **Reject**: Deny the request (you can add a comment).

### 4. Reports
- **Monthly Report**: View aggregated attendance data for your team for any specific month.
- **Yearly Report**: View annual attendance summaries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I see attendance for employees outside my team?**
A: No, you only have access to data for employees assigned to you.

**Q: How do I assign a new member to my team?**
A: Currently, team assignments are managed by Administrators. Please contact HR or an Admin to update team structures.

**Q: Do I need to manually calculate working hours?**
A: No, the system automatically calculates working hours when an employee checks out.
